# Decision log

Owner decisions made during the build, in order. Each one updates the relevant spec module or the open-decision register (SOS-28).

## 2026-09-19

**Design law: one hero, one number, one action.** Every role screen shows one hero card, one primary number, and one primary action above the fold on a phone. Everything else lives behind a segmented control (Now / Queue for reps, with Me as its own tab; Now / Money / Source for the owner, with Team as its own tab) or a Sheet. Explanatory sentences are cut to labels. Applies to SOS-09, SOS-10, SOS-20, SOS-21 implementations.

**Gamification is first-class, evidence-only.** XP comes only from verified stage events (two-way contact, retained booking, attended show, verified fit, signed, cash collected, accepted handoff, completed practice). Three tracks: commercial, mastery, team. Levels, monthly season, streaks tolerant of approved leave, one active mission with its proof rule. Quality incidents pause the mechanic per rep. Implemented in `src/domain/game.ts`. Never converts to pay (SOS-15, D06).

**Automatic stage capture is the product.** Each A to B transition has a declared evidence source and a confirm-only-when-ambiguous rule. See the table in `docs/PRD.md` section 10.

**Call intelligence, not a third-party notetaker.** Record through the business-owned dialer, transcribe, extract with a fixed schema (outcome, commitments, stakeholders, objections, next step) where every field cites a transcript span. The policy engine moves the stage; the model proposes. Reps can dispute any extracted field. Phase 7, blocked on D04 and D05.

**Warm-lead power dialer.** Queue ordered by promise time, reply urgency, lead freshness. One tap dials the next lead through the business number, consent checked at dial time, lease prevents double dialing. Auto-advance mode later. Never cold or random numbers. Phase 7, blocked on D04 and D05.

**Native by role.** A person signs in as who they are and never sees another role's world. Reps: Today, Team, Me. Owner: Business, Team, Me. Three tabs, no role switcher, no page headers or subtitles, no persona pickers. Learning the app should take zero explanation. Applies to the shell and every route.

**Test from every angle.** Automated journeys per role, not page screenshots: setter lead to handoff, closer join to verbal yes with zero collected, owner business to bottleneck assignment, rep blocked from owner routes. Run on every build.

**Leads from anywhere, one intake.** A universal intake layer normalizes any source (Meta and Google lead forms, VSL and landing page webhooks, calendar bookings, share links for organic and DMs, manual, Zapier and Make, email forward, CSV) into one LeadSubmission with source and entry path stamped, central dedupe and consent capture. Owner gets a Sources screen with per-source health and a three-step add flow. Implemented in `src/domain/intake.ts`.

**OAuth first, always.** For every connection and every data transfer, the first option is Connect, authorize in the tool they already use, done. Files, API keys, and webhooks exist only for tools that offer no OAuth, and appear below the OAuth option, never beside it. Applies to Connect, Import, and any future sync. Implemented in `src/domain/crmSync.ts` and the Connect and Import screens.

**Every brand gets its real logo.** Wherever another company is named, its actual mark is shown, never a letter tile. Simple Icons silhouettes painted in brand color where available; otherwise the official favicon or a color SVG rendered as-is. Generic items (share link, spreadsheet) use a neutral icon. Registry: `logo` override on each provider in `src/domain/integrations.ts`.

**Bring existing data in without friction.** An Import flow matches an old CRM export or spreadsheet to our schema by header and value shape, with exact presets for HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, Close, and Google Sheets. The owner reviews only the columns we are unsure about, sees a dry run before anything writes, and every imported record carries its source row. Imported consent never overwrites a newer opt-out. Implemented in `src/domain/migration.ts` and the owner `/import` screen.

**Personality routing signals.** Customer's own words and texting style: allowed as hypotheses that a real exchange must confirm (SOS-07 lens library). One explicit question in the funnel ("Numbers first, or see how it works?") is the primary pairing signal. Profile-picture or appearance inference: rejected. Unreliable and a legal exposure; the spec already excludes it (SOS-07, SOS-23).

**Make it fun: profiles and cards.** First sign-in builds a profile: photo, the name they go by, a handle, one line on how they sell, a color. The owner builds the business profile: logo, name, timezone. Photos replace initials everywhere a name appears. Tapping an avatar opens a rep card: photo, level ring, tier badge, streak, this season's verified numbers, and the pair they run with. Profiles are presentation only: the "how I sell" line is self-description and never feeds routing, eligibility, or pay (SOS-07); roles stay owner-controlled on User; photos are tenant-private. Model in `src/domain/profile.ts`; storage through `ProfileStore` (memory now, Supabase storage later).

**The transcript decides. No rep approval.** Every call is scored per stage by the model (contacted, qualified, buying, bought), each as a probability with a band: 80 and up Yes, 63 to 79 Likely, 40 to 62 Unlikely, under 40 No; bands are owner settings. Stages move automatically when the score clears the band; below it the stage shows Leaning with the number. Reps never confirm; Dispute remains as an untouched escape hatch. Money, consent, and attendance still come only from the ledger and providers. The extraction contract is provider-agnostic and schema-validated so any reasoning model can run it, always with span citations. A lens pack (ego archetypes, the owner's sales frameworks, offer facts, do-nots) is read on every call so feedback, hints, and angles come through the owner's lens. This supersedes the "confirm if unsure" rule in the stage capture table (SOS-09 step 6, SOS-13 review states) and is recorded in SOS-28 as an owner decision.

**Nothing bordered unless it is tappable.** Added after the review and post-call screens grew tiles and chips. Stage scores render as one slim bar, never four tiles. A screen shows one word, one ring or number, one bar, one button; every field, moment, and reference lives behind a single Details tap. Chips are for state that changes what the person does next, not for restating the hero.

**Positioning: a measurable, self-improving game.** From the competitive landscape study (`docs/POSITIONING.md`). Three changes adopted: say "standardized and auditable," never "objective," and surface verified fit beside perceived fit with the perception gap as coaching data; show Buyer Mode (decision speed, evidence preference, risk sensitivity, control orientation, detail appetite, social proof need, primary motivation, primary friction, style, each with confidence and cited words) on the closer brief instead of archetype labels, archetypes staying an internal lens; sit on the customer's existing CRM by OAuth first and earn system of record. Pricing is value-based, exclusivity lives in distribution only, and the model is an ingredient under our own data.

**Label archetypes, with the percentage.** Archetype reads are shown, never hidden, as "Competence 72%" with the prospect's cited words, so reps see what the model believes and how strongly. No archetype is ever a verdict; the number is the honesty. Reads accumulate across calls.

**The read is one word.** On the closer brief: a tiny "They value" kicker, the value word as the biggest text on the card (Proof, Control, Certainty, Growth, Standing out, A known contact, Reassurance, Their people, Their family, What's new, Simplicity, Something lasting), the archetype label with its percentage as a small subheader, "Tap for details". Details lists all twelve alphabetically with percentage, confidence word, and cited words. Map in `src/domain/buyerMode.ts` (VALUE_WORD).

**Word choice is significance.** A prospect could have said anything and said it that way: first mention counts. Said again, it is their language: repetition raises the weight and the vocabulary list shows the count. When we pitch, we speak their domain. Baseball stays baseball; the model never substitutes another analogy or a generic synonym, and every suggested angle is in the prospect's own frame. Implemented in `src/domain/references.ts` (occurrence weighting, vocabulary), the lens prompt, and the closer brief.

**Money forward.** A rep's own commission for the month is the Me hero: one tier mark, one number, one action (Collect more). Five tiers by monthly commission, coins, cash, stacks, bags, diamonds, with owner-configurable thresholds (`TierPolicy` in `src/domain/cashTiers.ts`, pilot default tiers-1.0 at $1,000, $5,000, $25,000, $100,000). Collected cash drops as falling tier icons, once, and not under reduced motion. Accrued, eligible, and paid are always shown as three separate figures beside the total. Teammates show a tier badge and net collected cash on the race and the board, never their commission dollars (SOS-14, D11). Every commission figure carries the Hypothetical policy chip until a real agreement lands (D06). Tiers and animation are display only and never touch pay, eligibility, or routing.

**Pairs are relational.** Appointment setters and closers work as pairs (Batman and Robin). A `Pair` entity records who chose the pairing (`chosenBy`: closer, setter, or owner), when it started and ended, and whether it is active. Every opportunity that reaches a closer through a setter carries the `pairId` that was active at handoff acceptance, so the two people's metrics converge on the same opportunities. The pair funnel has a setter side (assigned, two-way contact, booked, retained, show), a handoff (accepted of total, hours to accept), and a closer side (perceived qualified, won, net collected cash). The pair diagnostic compares each connector to the pooled rate across the tenant's pairs (sum over sum, never a mean), ignores denominators under 5, and names the side and the stage, never the person; it lists upstream possibilities (lead mix, handoff timing, fit evidence, data state) and suggests an owner (setter, closer, both, or sales ops when the data is not complete). Pair rankings (net collected per assigned opportunity, provisional under the matured minimum, tied ranks shared) are a separate board and never mix with individual boards. Individual rankings and tier icons are per role with separate thresholds: setter coins $0, cash $500, stacks $2,500, bags $10,000, diamonds $25,000 (`tiers-setter-1.0`); closer coins $0, cash $1,000, stacks $10,000, bags $30,000, diamonds $100,000 (`tiers-closer-1.1`, owner-set, also the default policy); all owner-configurable. Setters and closers never race each other: `cashRace` is role-scoped. Commission rates are per role in the hypothetical policy (`CommissionPolicy.role`): 5% setter, 10% closer, on the same net collected cash, until a real agreement lands (D06). Implemented in `src/domain/pairs.ts` and `src/domain/cashTiers.ts`; fixture pairs and role policies in `src/fixtures/obavia.ts`.

**The front end is the product. Nothing on a screen but the hero, one number, one action, and the list.** Owner directive after the dark-mode audit of 2026-09-19: people value ease, and every extra block reads as stress. So a screen is at most four things: one hero card (a name or a time, one muted line, one primary action), one segmented control if the screen has two views, one list, and the tab bar. Every other fact (stats strips, level and streak, pair funnel and diagnostic, race, quality gate, unknowns, fit chips, why-you, evidence tags, accrued and eligible and paid, tier thresholds, funnel tiles, cohort captions, source-sheet toggles) moves behind a single tap: a Details sheet, a chevron row, or the tapped item's own sheet. No card carries more than one number. Chips appear only for state that changes what the person does next; provenance tags (Verified, Customer-stated, AI-proposed) live inside the Details sheet, never on the screen. The closer brief opens on the read (They value, the value word, the archetype with its percentage) and the approach, and everything else is behind Details. Dark is the default theme and every screenshot sent to the owner is taken in dark. Hidden content stays reachable and tested; it is not deleted.

**A held measurement never holds the person.** Owner review of the dark screens, 2026-09-19. Read together, the screens said "resolve problems and wait for things to unlock" when they should say "sell, improve, compete". So a data incident freezes only what it actually makes unreliable, and everything downstream of it keeps running. An unlinked payment makes attribution-dependent revenue, commission, and standings provisional; calling, appointments, transcript coaching, and unrelated verified activity continue untouched. Missing attendance evidence holds that meeting's attendance result and the metrics that depend on it; other meetings and lead work continue. A communication read without evidence is tentative or absent; the brief, the known facts, and the conversation continue. A genuine contact restriction blocks the prohibited action with its reason and nothing else. The system reconciles what it can, routes the exception to its owner, and states the limited effect; it never says progress is paused because something somewhere is wrong. Coaching is never suppressed wholesale: transcript-based coaching stands while revenue-based recommendations wait. Implemented as a scope on each incident in `src/domain/incidents.ts`, consumed by `game.ts`, `leaderboard.ts`, and `coaching.ts`.

**Game words for the game, money words for money.** A tier name is never the label on a commission figure. The rep's money reads as money with its real status under the commission policy (earned, pending, payable) and its period; the tier mark, the level, and the race live in the game section where the rules are stated. Every primary action names its destination: what "Collect more" opens is written on it. Nobody should have to decode their own compensation.

**A stage is a stage, a prediction is a prediction, a payment is a payment.** The model still scores every stage and still moves the stage automatically when the score clears the band (the mechanic in "The transcript decides" is unchanged). What changes is the surface. A default view states the current assessment in words ("Considering the offer"), offers "View supporting conversation", and names the next step. A bare number with a ring is not shown on a default view, because a percentage with no stated meaning, horizon, or track record reads as more certainty than the evidence carries. The number, what it is a probability of, and how it has performed against real outcomes live one tap in. A numeric purchase probability returns to a default view only after it is calibrated against outcomes.

**The read is a suggested approach first.** The closer brief leads with what to do ("Lead with evidence") and says plainly that it is a tentative recommendation from an earlier conversation. Archetype labels and their percentages stay, one tap in, each with the sentence that says what the percentage is and the customer's own words under it. This refines "Label archetypes, with the percentage" and "The read is one word": the labels are still shown and never hidden, they no longer headline a screen without their explanation.

**Never show a list that looks ranked when ranking is not established.** When standings are provisional or unavailable, the list says so and is presented as a roster in a stated order, not as a silent ranking. A rep's own rank and the team board never contradict each other. A verified zero and a missing figure never look alike: "$0 collected" and "payment data not available" are different words in different weights.

**A pair screen answers what we owe each other.** A pair is named by the work it shares, never by a bare adjective. "Behind" is replaced by the specific thing: a handoff overdue by its hours, or a figure against its target with the gap. Opening a pair answers two questions before any comparison: what are we responsible for together (handoffs waiting on acceptance, shared opportunities coming up, our results through the stages we both touch) and what does each of us do next. Coordination first, competition on top.

**Today connects to improvement.** Under the appointment and the queue, one compact area, never a dashboard: during the working day, today's verified progress through conversations, bookings, and attended appointments; after a reviewed call, the single next improvement with a link straight to the moment in the transcript that shows it. A rep should never visit three sections to learn what to do, why it matters, and whether they are getting better.

**Every correction keeps the original.** Disputing a model assessment is "Flag an issue", not "Wrong?", and it preserves the original extraction, the cited spans, and the correction history as events. An observation anywhere in the product links to the passage it came from; a conclusion never sits above a transcript that the reader has to search themselves.

**Say the whole measurement.** Every ratio carries its denominator in words (per assigned opportunity, not "per assigned"), every figure carries its period, and anything provisional says so where it is read. Decisive information is never truncated: what is missing, who resolves it, and what it affects stay visible. Secondary text is legible on a phone at normal brightness, and any badge small enough to need memorizing carries its meaning in text on tap.

**Onboarding from zero.** Identity is OAuth first: Google, work email magic link, or phone one-time code, never a password. The owner signs in and creates the business (name, timezone, currency), which creates the tenant and the owner membership. Reps come in by email invite, text invite, or a six-character team code (no 0, O, 1, or I) shown as a QR; the owner sets the role and an optional partner on the invite, and a rep never chooses their own role. Email and text invites expire in 7 days, team codes in 24 hours, and any invite can be revoked before it is accepted. An identity that already belongs to another business gets a new membership, never a merge: one person can belong to several businesses and sessions are per business (`switchTenant`). Removal keeps the User and every opportunity, event, and commission entry; only `membership.active` flips and `removedAt` is stamped. Every screen is honest with zero data (`emptyStateFor`): no fake zeros, no placeholder charts, and each empty state points at the next step; coaching stays empty until 25 opportunities. The owner checklist runs Business profile, Connect a source, Invite your team, First lead, First appointment, First cash. Calls are reviewed natively: recorded through the business-owned dialer or a Zoom or Google Meet recording the business connects itself (borrowed capture), transcribed, extracted with span citations, and disputable field by field; no third-party notetaker. Model in `src/domain/onboarding.ts`; auth behind `AuthAdapter` in `src/data/auth.ts` (simulated and deterministic in the pilot, Supabase Auth skeleton for production, D04).
