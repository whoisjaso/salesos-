## Owner decisions that cap several of these rows

Five policy defaults are implemented as named constants and none is ratified. Until the owner settles them, a row that depends on one cannot rise above its current grade:

1. **Attribution freeze point.** Identities are captured at handoff acceptance and sealed when the order is issued for payment. `ATTRIBUTION_FREEZE_POLICY`, `ratified: false`.
2. **Net collected cash basis.** Processor-confirmed live movements only. Fees excluded and reported separately, open disputes at risk with no debit, refunds after a period closes restated and disclosed. `NET_COLLECTED_CASH_POLICY`, `ratified: false`.
3. **Commission rates.** 5 percent setter and 10 percent closer, still marked hypothetical pending D06. The specification's worked example uses different numbers and is labelled assumptions for that example only. It must never overwrite the recorded decision.
4. **Historical import window.** 90 days, owner-overridable. `HISTORICAL_IMPORT_DEFAULT_WINDOW_DAYS`.
5. **Shared merchant policy.** A second workspace connecting an already-bound merchant is rejected with a reason, never resolved by email match. `MERCHANT_BINDING_CONFLICT_POLICY`, `ratified: false`.

## What no grade on this page establishes

- No Stripe, Whop or Toast endpoint was called, in any environment.
- No application registration, granted scope, provider approval or account eligibility was inspected. Provider readiness is a release gate, not an assumed fact.
- No payment SDK is installed, so no method was validated against an installed release.
- The Supabase migration has never been executed. Every claim about a constraint is a claim about SQL text.
- Fixtures are synthetic and labelled synthetic. The source sheet behind this product is a visual reading, not verified operating data.
